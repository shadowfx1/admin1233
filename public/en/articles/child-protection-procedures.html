Child Abuse: Child Protection Law and Required Procedures

29 January 2026 · 6 min read

Child abuse cases demand careful and prompt handling because of their impact on the child and the family. Dealing with them under the Child Protection Law safeguards the child's interest and rights. This article is general awareness and not advice on a specific case.

The Child Protection Law

The Child Protection Law in Saudi Arabia governs the handling of abuse and neglect cases. It aims to protect children from all forms of violence and abuse and sets procedures for reporting, intervention and follow-up, based on the principle that the child's best interest underlies every measure.

Procedures when abuse occurs

These typically include:

  • Reporting the situation to the competent authorities as early as possible.
  • Documenting evidence, such as photographs, medical reports and messages.
  • Protecting the child from any further harm during proceedings.
  • Following the investigation and representation before the competent authorities.

The role of the family and the lawyer

The family plays an important role in protecting the child through reporting and cooperation with the competent authorities. The lawyer clarifies rights and procedures, represents the child or guardian and pursues claims for rights and compensation, while respecting the child's privacy at every step.

Family aspects and custody

Abuse cases may be linked to custody or care procedures under the Personal Status Law. In such cases prior legal advice is advisable, because decisions concerning a child require careful study of their best interest.

Conclusion

Protecting a child begins with timely reporting and continues with legal advice at every stage. The Rayat Al Qanoon team handles these cases in strict confidence and with the child's best interest guiding every step.
